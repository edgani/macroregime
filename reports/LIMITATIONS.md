# Limitations and Data Debt

## What this build proves

- The EROS v3 application architecture runs with five main decision tabs.
- Synthetic mode and execution lock are explicit and testable.
- Core PIT, data-health, narrative-firewall, competing-thesis, mechanism-graph, conservative-EV, waiting, exposure, scenario, registry, and replay contracts exist.
- The repository can represent global markets and additional mechanisms through registries.

## What this build does not prove

- No economic mechanism is universally true.
- No bundled candidate is approved for live capital.
- No live global point-in-time data estate is complete.
- No expected-return or probability output has sufficient prospective calibration.
- No private portfolio data, tax situation, broker access, borrow, or liquidity constraints are loaded.
- The synthetic dashboard is not current market analysis.

## Material data debt

1. Legal, versioned adapters for official global macro and policy sources.
2. Point-in-time corporate filings and local-market metadata, including IDX.
3. Independent physical-flow, funding, positioning, and capital-flow families.
4. Frozen multi-country positive, negative, failed-theme, and regime holdouts.
5. A sealed prospective prediction journal with matured outcomes.
6. Calibration, model-decay, replication, and false-discovery reporting.
7. A private, isolated portfolio store and real execution-friction model.

## Governance constraint

Missing evidence must remain `UNKNOWN`, `DATA_DEBT`, `STALE`, or `NO_DATA`. It must not be converted into a neutral score or a persuasive narrative.
