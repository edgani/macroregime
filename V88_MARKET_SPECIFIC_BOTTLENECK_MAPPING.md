# V8.8 Market-Specific Bottleneck → Price Projection Mapping

## Core rule

A bottleneck is not a buy signal.  War Room must separately prove:

1. the bottleneck is real;
2. the instrument is the actual value recipient;
3. supply cannot close the gap inside the decision horizon;
4. monetisation or a dated activation event has started;
5. expectations and valuation still lag;
6. the value bridge produces a conservative, base and upside scenario;
7. blind historical and prospective outcomes show the target range is calibrated;
8. realized trades pass profit-factor, drawdown, costs and capacity gates.

Current price is allowed only as a valuation denominator and execution reference.  Historical chart patterns are not inputs.

## US stocks

The projection must convert capacity, customer qualification, lead time, pricing power, regulatory approval or supplier concentration into revenue, margin or free cash flow.  The target is calculated per diluted share after net debt, non-operating assets and expected dilution.  The reason must state which customer/order/capacity path creates each unit of incremental value and what would invalidate it.

## IHSG

The equity value bridge is similar to US equities, but the bottleneck map must also include controller supply, historical free float, broker inventory persistence, crossings, import dependency, concessions and corporate actions.  Broker flow cannot substitute for beneficial ownership proof.  The target remains an operating-value projection per diluted share, not a broker-code story.

## Commodities

The quoted price is bridged from marginal supply cost, inventory-cover scarcity rent, quality/location basis, freight/insurance and policy effects.  The engine must identify the exact grade, delivery point and contract horizon.  A physical deficit without storage, location and supply-response mapping is insufficient.

## FX

The target begins from a pair-specific fundamental anchor and applies separately frozen adjustments for the policy path, balance of payments, terms of trade, global liquidity, funding stress and intervention.  The quote convention must be explicit because a positive adjustment means a higher quoted exchange rate, not universally a stronger currency.

## Crypto

The target must connect a protocol or market-structure bottleneck to token-required value capture.  Annual fees/revenue, treasury value, monetary premium, liabilities and projected diluted supply are explicit.  Unlocks and token dilution cannot be ignored.  Funding, OI and liquidations are amplification only.

## Proof rule across markets

Each market is independently tested.  A US-equity result cannot validate IHSG; an oil result cannot validate metals; a Deribit result cannot validate every crypto venue.  The global War Room is only called final when all five market receipts independently pass their exact scope.
