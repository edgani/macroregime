# V6.0 SNDK causal reconstruction — what should have been visible before the surge

## Claim boundary

This is a timestamped causal reconstruction and acquisition blueprint, not a completed SNDK backtest. The bundled equity panel ends before the 2025–2026 episode and contains no fresh point-in-time analyst, borrow, NAND contract-price or customer-qualification history. Live weight remains zero.

## Observable chain

1. **14 August 2025 baseline expectation:** Sandisk guided fiscal Q1 revenue to $2.10–$2.20 billion.
2. **6 November 2025 expectation break:** reported $2.31 billion, 21% sequential growth and above guidance; Datacenter grew 26%. Management disclosed two hyperscalers in qualification plus a third hyperscaler and a top storage OEM planned for calendar 2026.
3. **29 January 2026 persistence:** Q2 revenue reached $3.03 billion, 31% sequential growth; Datacenter grew 64%, linked to AI infrastructure builders and deployments at scale.
4. **30 April 2026 value-capture confirmation:** Q3 revenue reached $5.95 billion, 97% sequential growth; Datacenter grew 233%, pricing rose, and Q4 guidance moved to $7.75–$8.25 billion. The 10-Q attributed the expansion to both ASP per gigabyte and exabytes sold.

## What the early detector must measure

- analyst revenue/EPS revision velocity and breadth;
- guidance-versus-consensus gap;
- NAND contract and spot pricing, inventory and capacity utilization;
- hyperscaler qualification stage and conversion to volume orders;
- datacenter exabyte shipment and ASP inflection;
- gross-margin sensitivity to ASP versus cost per gigabyte;
- customer mix and new business-model agreements;
- supplier/customer read-through;
- short interest, borrow fee and option/flow only as vulnerability/transmission layers.

## What probably did not originate the move

Total OI, realized liquidation, generic volume surge and technical momentum may have amplified or confirmed the repricing, but they do not explain why earnings power changed.

## Frozen research target

At each historical filing/estimate date, predict whether a stock will enter the top-K future residual-return set while retaining at least 50% of the eventual move. Compare expectation/bottleneck features against price-volume, momentum, valuation and sector baselines; require validation, untouched lockbox, lead time, remaining return, MAE and prospective evidence.
