# V10.1 Carry Method References

- BIS, *Sizing up carry trades in BIS statistics* (2024): carry is leveraged cross-currency exposure to rate differentials and low volatility; available statistics are indirect and must be interpreted cautiously.
- BIS, *Covered interest parity lost* (2016): cross-currency basis changes the effective funding cost and cannot be ignored when available.
- CFTC, *About the COT Reports*: Tuesday positions are released Friday; V10.1 applies publication-lag semantics.
- CME FX Swap Rate Monitor: tradable FX swap points can be used to estimate implied interest-rate differentials when the route is available.
- Bank Indonesia BI-Rate and SNB current-rates pages are official current policy anchors. Historical proof requires archived decision-date records.

No source is treated as proof that carry earns positive returns. Proof is outcome-based and must pass the V9.6 anti-overfit gate plus prospective shadow and actual-fill gates.
