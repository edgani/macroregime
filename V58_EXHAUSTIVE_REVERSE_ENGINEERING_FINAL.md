# War Room OS V5.8 — Exhaustive Reverse-Engineering and Trial Accounting

## Scope

V5.8 does not rank ideas by how convincing they sound. It maps and retains predictors, placebos, dropped signals, weak theses, controversial mathematical models, technical/price-volume variants, macro interactions, options, microstructure, commodities, FX, crypto, IHSG structure, networks, bottlenecks, and portfolio constructions.

This is a versioned and extensible research universe, not a claim that every unpublished or proprietary idea worldwide is knowable.

## Global accounting result

After one-sided Bonferroni correction across all 795 V5.8 claims, only these aggregate-return candidates retain positive gross validation and lockbox lower bounds:

1. AnalystRevision
2. AnnouncementReturn
3. DivYieldST

None survives the same global gate after a coarse 25 basis points per month cost stress. ShortInterest survived the narrower 212-family gross gate but failed the global validation lower bound.

These three are replication-queue candidates only. Maintained aggregate portfolios do not expose point-in-time constituents, true turnover, spread, market impact, borrow, capacity, stale estimates, identifier errors, or data-vintage revisions.

## Important contamination risk

AnnouncementReturn had a documented look-ahead bug in an earlier Open Source Asset Pricing implementation. The 2025 official release states that the bug was fixed, but V5.8's wide return file came from a public mirror and is only hash-pinned; it is not an independently authenticated official vintage. AnnouncementReturn therefore cannot be promoted without stock-level reconstruction from exact point-in-time announcement timestamps and returns.

## Negative evidence

The broad price/volume sweep found zero survivor after correction. Some placebo variants looked attractive by point estimate, demonstrating why unlogged trial-and-error creates false discoveries.

The macro sweep also found zero survivor after correction. This does not prove every macro mechanism is useless; it rejects the specific frozen formulas, interactions, targets, and historical panel tested here.

The public maintained AQR families showed directional persistence in many series, but none passed the strict simultaneous validation and lockbox gate used in V5.8.

## Placebo and alternative-construction boundary

All 114 placebo and 5 dropped definitions are mapped. Their precomputed wide return series were not found in the public release catalog available to this environment. The official project documents stock-level PlacebosIndiv files and an executable portfolio engine, but many raw inputs require WRDS or equivalent licensed point-in-time data.

Official maintainer download packages document alternative implementations including equal/value-weighted deciles, quintiles, NYSE-only, market-cap liquidity screens, and price-above-$5 screens. The archives were not acquired in the current runtime, so no claim is made that the three gross candidates survive those constructions.

No predictor series was substituted for a placebo series, and no synthetic alternative-portfolio result was manufactured.
