# Research and article import policy

## Default rule

External articles, videos, papers, posts and marketing claims may propose a hypothesis or validation method. They do not enter production as a formula, weight, trade direction, target, probability or performance claim.

## Allowed imports

- causal mapping and explicit invalidation;
- data-lineage and point-in-time controls;
- baselines, negative controls and planted positive controls;
- nonstationarity/feedback diagnostics;
- purged/embargoed walk-forward design;
- multiple-testing and discovery-ledger accounting;
- execution/microstructure research within its actual horizon;
- challenger models in a frozen tournament.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- copying proprietary signal counts or weights;
- converting breadth or positioning context into a forced trade;
- treating option OI as signed dealer inventory;
- generalizing short-horizon order-book relationships into swing direction;
- using one bottleneck formula across markets with different control points and value-capture mechanisms;
- promoting a paper result without independent replication;
- selecting only surviving trials or resetting the trial budget;
- retuning an opened lockbox;
- treating marketing performance as audited evidence.

## Specific treatment reflected in this package

- Wasserstein HMM remains a challenger, not a winner.
- IC, neutralization, DSR, correlation and decay belong to a scope-specific validation protocol; DSR must use the real discovery ledger.
- Order-flow imbalance remains execution/microstructure context unless separately proven at the claimed horizon.
- Chokepoint analysis maps resource → controller → substitution → transmission → value capture → lag → invalidation; it is not a universal score.
- Nicholas Crown-style sequential gates, early/late state and direction/option-structure separation may inform process; proprietary signals, weights, dealer claims, ticker calls and performance claims are excluded.
- Current crypto/company developments enter a timestamped event registry and structural map only.
- No Aleks Rosme technique is imported without a complete, auditable source record.
- Real Wealth / Poverty Wedge is outside the core War Room scope.
