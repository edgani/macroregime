# V5.8 Research Ingestion Rules

The research universe is deliberately extensible. It is not a claim that every unpublished or proprietary thesis in existence is known.

## Discovery sandbox

Any thesis may enter, including implausible, contradictory, placebo, failed-paper, marketing, technical-analysis, complex-systems, or proprietary-description candidates. Discovery may vary formulas, parameters, horizons, interactions, and markets. Every evaluated claim receives a permanent trial-ledger row.

## Evidence boundary

A candidate advances only after its claim, data lineage, formula, costs, baseline, split, correction family, and invalidation are frozen before validation outcomes are opened. Validation and lockbox are one-time evidence, not optimization data.

## Required candidate mapping

Each candidate records its decision purpose, causal role, source market and liquidity, stock/flow/surprise/state classification, transmission path, target and horizon, data lineage, interaction conditions, invalidation, and claim ceiling.

## Negative controls

Placebos, direction flips, future shifts, randomized labels, economically impossible signs, and deliberately weak baselines remain in the same global trial budget. Negative controls are not deleted when they accidentally appear profitable.

## Promotion

A candidate cannot receive live decision weight merely for historical persistence. It must survive an updated global multiple-testing budget, realistic cost/capacity/borrow/latency tests, strong baselines, untouched data, ablation, current-regime replication, and signed prospective observations. Human approval remains mandatory.

## Stop rules

A family is stopped when upstream identification or incremental prediction fails on multiple independent frozen studies. A new study after failure must use genuinely new data or a materially different causal claim preregistered before outcomes; changing thresholds or horizons on the same lockbox is prohibited.
