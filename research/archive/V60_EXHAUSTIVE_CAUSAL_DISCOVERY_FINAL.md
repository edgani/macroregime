## What changed versus the OI/liquidation question

Open interest, funding, liquidations, COT, options and broker inventory remain useful observable layers, but they answer narrower questions:

1. Was risk being added or removed?
2. Which side was forced to cover or liquidate?
3. Is a move being amplified mechanically?
4. Is positioning crowded or still under-owned?

They do not, by themselves, identify the underlying catalyst or prove the next direction. The research scope is therefore a full chain:

**fundamental or structural inflection → expectation revision → ownership/position build → price/liquidity confirmation → forced flow/reflexivity → exhaustion/distribution**

## Data-ready price/volume falsification

The bundled fixed 483-stock panel was used to test 131 predeclared features and every signed univariate and pairwise additive-rank combination. The target was not average return; it was an extreme move.

Targets:

- At least +30% within the following 63 trading days.
- Top 5% future maximum return in the monthly cross-section.
- At most −20% within the following 63 trading days.

Tested families included momentum, acceleration, trend quality, volatility and compression, skew/kurtosis, distance from highs/lows, relative strength, volume acceleration, dollar-volume liquidity, Amihud illiquidity, signed-volume proxies, accumulation/distribution proxies, gaps, intraday pressure, beta and idiosyncratic momentum.

Result: no candidate passed the frozen validation/global-correction/diagnostic-lockbox gate for any of the three targets. This is evidence against treating OHLCV pattern mining as a sufficient SNDK-like discovery engine.

Limitations remain material: the panel is short, fixed-universe and likely survivorship-biased, and lacks point-in-time fundamentals, revisions, ownership, derivatives and supply-chain data.

## Exhaustive factor-pair battery

All unordered pairs among 212 maintained Open Source Asset Pricing predictor-return series were tested in all four sign quadrants: **89,464 trials**.

The first pass produced 572 gross historical candidates. A follow-up robustness challenge applied, without discretionary candidate selection:

- complete validation and 2020–2024 windows;
- raw and winsorized Newey–West tests;
- 25/50/100 bps monthly cost stresses;
- annual and subperiod sign stability;
- rolling-window persistence;
- deletion of the best month;
- incremental risk-adjusted performance over both constituents;
- drawdown and correlation checks;
- a unified global correction across all 192,430 new trials.

Twenty-five remained research candidates. Recurring observable clusters were:

- analyst earnings revisions;
- institutional ownership interacting with short interest or idiosyncratic volatility;
- equity issuance, net external financing and net payout yield;
- profitability, investment and asset growth;
- firm age/IPO state and momentum;
- liquidity/trading-friction interactions.

These findings are useful for prioritizing point-in-time data acquisition. They are not proof of a stock-level algorithm because the input is maintained long-short portfolio returns, not historical ticker-by-ticker signals and investable universe membership.

## What a SNDK-like early winner engine must observe

A plausible causal chain for a capacity-constrained technology winner is:

1. Industry demand begins exceeding qualified supply.
2. Contract/spot pricing and product mix inflect.
3. Unit volume, ASP and gross-margin operating leverage improve.
4. Customer qualification, backlog or long-term purchase commitments extend persistence.
5. Management guidance and analyst estimates revise upward repeatedly.
6. Valuation and market capitalization remain below the reasonable state implied by the revised earnings path.
7. Ownership broadens without becoming fully crowded; financing/issuance does not absorb the upside.
8. Price/volume confirms information diffusion while sufficient remaining return still exists.

The War Room must reconstruct every item **as known on each historical date**. Reading later filings and explaining the winner afterward is not prediction.

## Bounded exhaustive-search grammar

Literal “every possible metric/formula” is infinite and would guarantee p-hacking. V6.0 therefore registers a bounded causal grammar rather than allowing arbitrary formulas after outcomes are viewed.

- Base mapped candidates: **868**
- Predeclared transformations: 10
- Horizons: 6
- Targets: 9
- Regimes: 11
- Bounded single-claim potential: **5,155,920**
- Naive pair-interaction potential: **5,364,219,168**

The billions of theoretical pair claims must not be blindly run on one dataset. They require hierarchical family tests, frozen discovery budgets and new untouched data. Otherwise at least some combinations will look spectacular by chance.

## Market-specific evidence requirements

### US stocks
Point-in-time filings, guidance, analyst estimates/revisions, earnings surprises, backlog, customer/supplier networks, capacity/pricing, ownership, borrow and short demand, insider/buyback/issuance, ETF flow, options surface and signed trade/order-book data.

### IHSG
Point-in-time issuer reports and disclosures, broker inventory persistence, foreign/domestic flow, crossing adjustment, free float/controller, corporate actions, commodity/FX sensitivity, customer/supplier mapping, order-book/done detail and available futures/SSF data.

### Commodities
Disaggregated COT/TFF, exchange OI/volume and signed flow, curve, options, inventory, physical differentials, freight/storage, production/consumption, capacity outages, weather and policy/geopolitical triggers.

### FX
TFF positioning, OIS/rate-path surprises, real-rate/value, cross-currency basis, options skew/term structure, reserves/intervention, foreign-currency debt mismatch, terms of trade and venue flow.

### Crypto
Venue-specific OI, funding/basis, signed liquidations, aggressor flow, order-book depth, options, on-chain exchange flows, stablecoin impulse, unlock/emission schedules, protocol usage/revenue and bridge/MEV flows.

No metric is transported across markets merely because its label sounds similar.

## Promotion rule

A candidate becomes usable only after:

1. exact point-in-time reconstruction;
2. survivorship-safe universe and delisting handling;
3. frozen claim, formula, target and horizon;
4. simple baseline and current War Room comparison;
5. globally corrected validation;
6. untouched lockbox;
7. calibration, false alarms, lead time, remaining return, MAE/MFE;
8. realistic liquidity, capacity, turnover and latency stress;
9. no-retuning replication across eras or relevant markets;
10. signed prospective trial.

Current result: **candidate discovery advanced; production proof remains absent.**
