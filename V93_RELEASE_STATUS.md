# War Room OS V9.3 — Public Bootstrap & Evidence Onboarding

## Verdict

V9.3 is **not trading-ready**. It is the first release that can automatically collect the public/official portion of the evidence plane and normalize genuine account fills without guessing broker semantics.

## What it solves

- Downloads and hashes SEC, CFTC, Binance and Deribit public evidence.
- Keeps raw source records separate from signals and outcomes.
- Accepts broker/exchange fills only through explicit column mappings.
- Rejects paper and synthetic fills.
- Separates public-automatable, licensed/export-required and user-account evidence.
- Keeps zero technical indicators.

## Why it cannot honestly be 5/5 yet

No model can manufacture:

1. survivor-free licensed histories where no complete public equivalent exists;
2. the user's genuine broker/exchange fills;
3. twenty-four months of prospective observations and four realized regimes.

Provider-ready is not market-proven. Market-proven is not limited-production-ready. Limited-production-ready is not fully proven.

## Required user/provider inputs

- US: Sharadar/CRSP-equivalent survivor-free bundle plus broker fills.
- IDX: official IDX historical/reference/fundamental exports plus Indonesian broker fills.
- Commodity and FX: CME historical data plus futures broker fills.
- Crypto: exchange-account fills; paid depth/on-chain history is needed for higher-capacity claims.

Capital permission remains `BLOCKED` until the frozen proof gates pass on real data and fills.
