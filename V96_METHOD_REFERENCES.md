# V9.6 Anti-Overfit Method Map

The software implements an internal approximation of the following research controls and does not claim to reproduce any paper verbatim:

- Combinatorially Symmetric Cross-Validation / Probability of Backtest Overfitting.
- Deflated Sharpe Ratio for selection bias and non-normal returns.
- White-style Reality Check / familywise bootstrap and Holm adjustment.
- Purged walk-forward with embargo to prevent overlapping-label leakage.
- Untouched lockbox frozen before outcomes.
- Parameter-neighbourhood, regime, cost, concentration, and chronology stability checks.

These controls reduce false discoveries; they cannot manufacture future evidence or guarantee profitability.
