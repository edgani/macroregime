## Proof-first activation policy

A component can exist in one of three active classes:

1. **Active operational validated** — may enforce data, lineage, capability, receipt, capital-zero, manifest, and trial-accounting controls. It has no predictive semantics.
2. **Evidence-active research, decision-inactive** — may display its exact supported claim and guide the next falsifier. It cannot rank tickers, set direction, set targets, size positions, or authorize capital.
3. **Decision-active predictive** — requires point-in-time reconstruction, capacity/liquidity, actual costs, untouched prospective evidence, and a signed exact-scope proof receipt.

Anything else remains quarantined.
