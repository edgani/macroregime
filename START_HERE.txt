EROS / WARROOM

Windows: double-click EROS.bat
Linux/macOS: ./run_eros.sh
CLI smoke run: python run.py
Force live refresh: python run.py --refresh

The app auto-fetches factual/public macro, Treasury, market context and GDELT news leads.
News leads are scenario discovery inputs ONLY and never become verified facts automatically.
Ticker candidates are generated automatically; BUY/SHORT remains fail-closed until PIT company transmission + calibration + priced-in + EV gates exist.
