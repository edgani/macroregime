## What was actually fixed

V9.0's old contracts were structurally contradictory: the predictor admission gate required outcomes that the manifest builder prohibited, expected a global decision time that the builder did not create, and rejected the absolute paths emitted by that builder. V9.1 makes the proof path executable by separating predictor and outcome custody, applying point-in-time checks per forecast, using portable relative paths, and scoring one exact market at a time.
