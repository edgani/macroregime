# What V6.6 Does Not Claim

V6.6 is not a declaration that War Room OS has universal or hedge-fund-beating alpha.

No method can honestly be certified as prospectively profitable across every market before its future forecasts mature. Repeatedly changing formulas until all components pass would manufacture false discoveries.

The production boundary is therefore explicit:

- One scoped historical risk-reduction control is decision-usable.
- Three US options/information modules remain aggregate archive evidence only.
- Every ticker and directional component remains zero-capital until point-in-time reconstruction, capacity/cost testing, an untouched prospective ledger, and an exact human-signed permission pass.

This is a stricter and more usable state than a dashboard full of unverified scores because the permitted action is known, testable, and cannot silently expand its scope.
