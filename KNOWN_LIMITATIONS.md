# Known limitations — v3.0

- v3.0 is a research/decision architecture, not a proven alpha model. Change scores are descriptive research states, not probabilities.
- Market Memory begins accumulating from deployment. Until enough snapshots exist, assets correctly show `BASELINE BUILDING`.
- DeFiLlama chain endpoints can be unavailable or renamed for newer ecosystems; errors are surfaced and never imputed bullish.
- On-chain wallet, social/narrative and developer-activity adapters are not universal yet. v3 does not infer these from TVL.
- Liquid crypto still lacks universal spot-flow, OI, funding and liquidation ingestion; leverage remains gated.
- US lacks full point-in-time analyst estimate-revision and institutional-flow history.
- IHSG broker attribution requires Index Alpha and intraday microstructure requires Invezgo. Missing keys fail closed.
- FX lacks a complete pair-specific relative-rate / REER / BoP / positioning model; price history alone cannot upgrade it.
- Commodities lack production physical balance / inventory / futures-curve adapters; price history alone cannot upgrade them.
- Market Memory outcome backfill machinery exists, but a broad historical PIT dataset is still required before analogues can be called predictive.
- Social/TikTok material is treated as a hypothesis source until its claims are converted into timestamped features and validated OOS.
